Privacy policy

What we collect through this website, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Last updated 7 November 2026

Who we are

V8 Motors Ltd, 59B Royal Road, Coromandel, Mauritius, is responsible for the personal data described here. We follow the Mauritius Data Protection Act 2017. Write to veightmotorsv8@gmail.com about anything in this policy.

What we collect

  • When you ask us to value your car: the make, model, year, mileage and condition, any photos you add, your name and mobile number.
  • When you send a message: your name, the phone number or email you give us, and your message.

We only use this to answer you, arrange a viewing or make an offer. We don’t sell it or share it for marketing. If you apply for leasing, we pass your application to Rogers Capital with your agreement, and only them.

What stays on your device

Your saved cars, whether you’ve seen the privacy notice and, if your device found the moving effects too heavy, a note to keep them lighter for three days are kept in your browser’s local storage. The cars you pick to compare are only kept while you browse: they are forgotten when you refresh the page or close it. None of this ever reaches us. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers. The map on the Visit us page is provided by Google and loads only on that page; Google’s own privacy policy applies to it.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don’t lead to a sale are deleted after 12 months. If you buy or sell a car, we keep the records the law requires for tax and vehicle registration, usually seven years.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email us and we’ll reply within one month. You can also complain to the Data Protection Office of Mauritius.
